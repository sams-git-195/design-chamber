# Editorial
**id:** editorial · **best for:** long-form reading, magazines, essays, brand storytelling with a voice

**Mood:** quiet authority, paper and ink, a confident point of view. Type leads; chrome recedes.

**Type:** display — high-contrast serif (Fraunces, Playfair Display, fallback Georgia); body — a companion serif or quiet grotesque (Source Serif 4, "Iowan Old Style", Georgia); mono for captions/metadata. Base 18px, scale ×1.25, body line-height 1.6, measure 62–68ch.

**Motifs:** eyebrow label above headlines, standfirst, drop cap, pull quotes, hairline rules, captioned figures, asymmetric 12-col layouts with a wide left margin, numbered sections.

**Do:** one warm accent used rarely; large headline vs small caption contrast; whitespace to separate; real article copy and bylines.
**Don't:** cards and shadows, centered text blocks, sans-serif everything, gradient buttons, more than one accent.
