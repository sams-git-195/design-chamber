# Warm Craft
**id:** warm-craft · **best for:** food, wellness, handmade/local brands, community and consumer apps with a human tone

**Mood:** hand-made, welcoming, tactile. Cream paper, earthy colour, soft shapes, friendly voice.

**Type:** display — soft serif ("Young Serif", "DM Serif Display", fallback Georgia); body — warm humanist sans (Nunito Sans, "Source Sans 3", fallback "Segoe UI"). Body 16–17px, line-height 1.55.

**Motifs:** subtle paper grain (inline SVG noise), hand-drawn underlines/arrows as inline SVG, rounded photo masks, stickers and labels, ingredient/material-style lists, warm duotone imagery.

**Do:** earthy secondaries (olive #6b7a3a, mustard #d9a441) sparingly; specific, friendly microcopy; roomy padding; touch-sized controls.
**Don't:** corporate blue, cold greys, empty stock-photo boxes, emoji as decoration.
